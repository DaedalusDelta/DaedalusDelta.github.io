# xHand interactive 3D optimization example

Open index.html to inspect six recorded states. Rotate, zoom, pan, select a state and toggle geometry or marker layers. viewer-generic.html can open another local trace.json.

The geometry, transforms, markers and 42 stored fit values are preserved. Private operational provenance is removed. Coordinates are right-handed XYZ in metres.

Five states use recorded cached transforms that may lag the integrated joint state. MAIN is a separate endpoint with eight native contact rows, distinct from seven planned rows. Missing data remains UNKNOWN. Stored fit differences are not physical clearance or contact force. Display meshes do not certify collision geometry, force closure or grasp retention. No intermediate iterations were fabricated.
